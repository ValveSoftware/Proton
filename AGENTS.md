# Repository instructions

## macOS handover rule

After completing any implementation unit related to Proton macOS support, update docs/MACOS_HANDOVER.md before reporting completion.

The handover update must include:

- UTC timestamp and source revision.
- What changed, with paths.
- Commands or tests run and their outcomes.
- New dependencies or environment changes.
- Decisions made and the evidence behind them.
- Known limitations, failures, and unresolved risks.
- The exact next recommended action.

Do not mark an implementation unit complete when its handover entry is missing or stale. Keep the newest entry first and preserve prior completed entries as an append-only history.
